The challenge

How can MTC recreate essential Qlik Analytics functionality in a customizable application, while integrating an AI-powered conversational experience through Qlik Answers?

Problem

Qlik's mobile app is built for dashboards. Its store listings and mobile help do not mention Qlik Answers. Qlik Answers runs in mobile browsers, but browser access was not an option for this project.

Solution

A native app that signs each person in to their own Qlik Cloud Tenant and brings Assistants and Analytics Apps into one tailored interface.

Value

One place to ask and to look. Answers cite their sources. Access stays governed by the permissions Qlik already enforces.

Security and access

Qlik controls access. Qurio protects the device.

Controls in the app

  • Each person signs in as themselves with OAuth 2.0 and PKCE. No shared account, client secret or MTC server.
  • The access token stays in memory. The refresh token is kept in the device's secure storage, on that device only.
  • Signing out revokes both tokens at Qlik, then clears them from the device.
  • Tokens are sent only to the configured Tenant.
  • Answers are not stored on the device. The embedded view runs privately with caching off.
  • Privacy lock on by default. Release builds request screenshot and recording protection.

Enforced by Qlik

  • Identity and login, including your identity provider
  • One-time approval of the app by a Tenant admin
  • Qlik Answers entitlement for each person
  • Access to spaces, Assistants and Knowledge Bases
  • Rate limits and service guardrails
In progressHardening of the embedded Qlik view (navigation, on-device storage and sign-out) and resolution of open dependency advisories.

Evaluation

Advantages, limitations and next improvements

Advantages Today

  • Native Qlik Answers on mobile, with sources
  • Every request runs as the signed-in person
  • No MTC server to run, patch or secure
  • No Answers kept on the device
  • Hostname-only setup, or device management
  • Interface and roadmap under MTC's control

Limitations Known

  • Analytics App viewer and Agentic Assistant are proofs of concept
  • Embedded views depend on Qlik behaviour without a formal contract
  • Sign-in clients for the AP and SG regions only
  • One Tenant at a time, online only, no alerts
  • Android beta only; no iOS release yet

Next Proposed

  • Production sign-in clients and more regions
  • Hardened embedded Qlik views
  • Sheet browsing inside Analytics Apps
  • Signed iOS build
  • Validation with a real device-management system
  • Fixed version of the Qlik embed component

Conclusion

Qurio is a customizable analytics experience powered by Qlik: governed answers and your Analytics Apps, in one native app.

Why

Qlik Answers on mobile without a browser, beside the apps people already use.

What works

Sign-in, Assistant chat with sources, conversation history, Apps browsing and device protection, in a signed Android beta.

How it fits

Direct to your Tenant through Qlik's APIs and embed component. Qlik keeps control of data and access.
